# Changelog

## 2.0

- Fist trails: punches leave trails from the knuckles, colored by your gauntlets' enchantment ("Fists" on the Settings page). They work with modded fist movesets too (MCO and others), follow the hand that actually punches and leave nothing while the arm pulls back.
- Trails take the color the enchantment glows with on the item: Fortify Health red, Fortify Stamina green, Fortify Magicka blue...
- Enchantment list on the Colors page: give any enchantment, from any mod, its own color or no trail.
- Soul trap ("of Binding") weapons now get the Soul trap color.
- VR: trails for the player too: every fast swing leaves one (VR section on the Settings page).
